How it works

You create an account with ASAP-Rank once. Then you put your own copy of the app on your own free Cloudflare account. Your pages, SEO changes, popups, chatbot and contacts stay there, not with us. Your copy asks ASAP-Rank only "is this licence active?" each time someone logs in. The background engine (Step 6) also runs in your own free Supabase and GitHub accounts. We only provide the software and the licence. It takes about 35 minutes in total, and you can do it in parts: this page remembers what you ticked.

Which address do I open? (read this first)

You will see several addresses. Only one of them is for daily use: the ASAP-Rank login page. Everyone, the Admin and every customer, uses the same login page.

AddressWhat it is
Login and sign-up page: the ASAP-Rank website address plus /auth, for example https://asap-rank.yourdomain.com/authUse this every day. Type your email there and it takes you to your own app. Bookmark it. Your Admin gave you the website address; it is also the one in "ASAP-Rank website address" above.
Your Worker address (ends in .workers.dev)The engine of your own copy. It shows no pages. If you open it in a browser it only sends you on to the login page. Do not bookmark it.

"Logging in…" never finishes, or it says it cannot reach your app? The login page cannot talk to your Worker. Check, in order: (1) In Cloudflare open your Worker, Settings, Domains and routes: workers.dev must say Enabled. If it says Disabled, turn it on; without it your Worker has no address. (2) Your Worker has all of these variables, and you pressed Deploy after adding them: LICENSE_KEY, HUB_URL, SECRET, SETUP_CODE and FRONT_URL. FRONT_URL is the ASAP-Rank website address; without it the login page is blocked from reaching your app. (3) The ASAP-Rank website itself opens: try its /auth page. If it does not, tell your Admin. The login page shows a clear message after 15 seconds instead of waiting forever.

Getting updates with one click

When a new version is released, the Install tab and the Home screen of your ASAP-Rank show A new version is ready, with what changed. Press Sync now: your own GitHub copies the new files after checking them against the owner's published checksum, and Cloudflare redeploys the app by itself. Your settings, data, wrangler.jsonc and site/config.js are never touched.

To switch this on, add these variables to your Cloudflare Worker (Settings, Variables), then press Deploy: GH_REPO (your runner repository, as name/repo), GH_TOKEN (a GitHub token that can write to your repositories) and GH_APP_REPO (your copy of the app repository). Also set FRONT_URL to your public site address: your Worker shows no pages itself, it only sends browsers there.

If you set up before this feature existed, replace .github/workflows/publish.yml once with the current one from Step 6b. A workflow file cannot update itself.

Quick setup (recommended): one command

After Step 2, this replaces Steps 3, 4 and 6. The installer makes one public GitHub repository (asap-runner; nothing private, no copy of the app repository), your Supabase project, and your Cloudflare database and app, then connects them and turns on free Pages. Needs Node.js 18 or newer (free, from nodejs.org).

  1. Make the three free accounts in Step 3 (GitHub, Cloudflare, Supabase). Email verification only, no card.

  1. It asks for three tokens and prints the exact page to open for each: GitHub (tick repo and workflow), Supabase, and Cloudflare (permissions: Workers Scripts Edit, D1 Edit, Account Settings Read). Paste each and press Enter.

Still by hand: creating the accounts, verifying emails, making the three tokens, your admin login and the snippet on your website. To update later, run the same command again. Step 5. Create your admin login

  1. Open your app address. Your Worker shows no pages itself: it sends your browser on to the ASAP-Rank login page (the address ends in /auth), and you see Create your admin login. If you see a 404 or a message about FRONT_URL, add FRONT_URL as described in "Getting updates with one click" above, press Deploy and try again.

  4. From now on log in with email or WhatsApp number and password at your app address.

Step 8. Get your first improvements

  1. Add an AI model: More, AI models. A free Google Gemini key from Google AI Studio works.
  2. In All URLs tick a few pages and press AI suggestions for selected, or press AI quick wins for the pages with the most problems first. Suggestions appear within a few minutes.
  3. Open a page. Read each suggestion, edit it if you like, then press Approve and Publish now. Revoke takes a change off your site again.

Step 9. Lead capture, messages and payment

  1. Settings, Messaging: connect your email service. Marketing: popups, chatbot, drip messages and CRM (all included in your plan).
  2. To activate your account: More, Plan, and pay with the details shown. Send the transaction ID by replying to the welcome email. Your app picks up the activation at your next login.

Step 10. Using ASAP-Rank every week

  1. Home: see how your checks score, page speed and search position changed this week and this month. The first comparison appears after a week.
  2. All URLs: import your sitemap, press Check all new, tick pages and press AI suggestions for selected (or AI quick wins).
  3. Open a page: edit, Approve, Publish now. Revoke removes a change again.
  4. Review: everything waiting in one list. Turn on Auto-publish for the safe types after a waiting time you choose.
  5. After publishing, wait a few minutes (up to about 10). The free files refresh in the background.
  6. Marketing: popups, chatbot, drip messages and CRM. Leads from popups and chat land in CRM.
  7. Once a month: re-import the sitemap for new pages, and upload the same Search Console export so search position shows progress.

Do and do not, by situation

Tap a situation to open it.

First setup
Do
  • Create your ASAP-Rank account first (Step 1) and copy your licence key (Step 2).
  • Fill in the address boxes at the top before you deploy.
  • Press Deploy again after you change any Cloudflare variable.
  • Tick each step when done: this page remembers your progress.
Do not
  • Do not type http:// anywhere.
  • Do not end an address with / or add /app to it.
  • Do not bookmark the Worker address (ends in workers.dev). It only sends you to the login page.
  • Do not share your licence key, Supabase key or GitHub token.
Addresses
Do
  • HUB_URL is the engine address your Admin gave you. FRONT_URL is your Admin’s website address. APP_URL is your own Worker address.
  • STATIC_BASE is your own github.io address.
  • Check https://your-github-address/rp.js in a browser: you must see JavaScript.
  • Use the login page (website address plus /auth) every day.
Do not
  • Do not mix them up: your Worker address is not the HUB_URL.
  • Do not use the github.io address as a login page.
  • Do not remove HUB_URL or LICENSE_KEY from your Worker: your app then cannot check your licence.
GitHub and publishing
Do
  • Upload the runner files with the site folder at the top level of your asap-runner repository.
  • Add the GitHub secrets exactly as listed, then run the publish workflow and wait for the green tick.
  • Open Settings, Pages and set the source to GitHub Actions.
Do not
  • Do not upload the files into a sub-folder. Old pages stay online when the files are in the wrong place.
  • Do not delete files in the docs folder by hand.
  • Do not make the repository private: Pages stops working.
The snippet on your website
Do
  • Copy a fresh snippet from the Install tab whenever an address changes.
  • Remove every old ASAP-Rank tag from your website or Tag Manager before you add the new one. In Tag Manager press Submit and Publish.
  • Open your website in a private window, then press Refresh status in the Install tab.
Do not
  • Do not keep two different snippets on one page.
  • Do not paste only part of the tag: the address and the data attributes belong together.
  • Do not use a snippet that starts with http://: the browser blocks it.
Updates
Do
  • Press Sync now when the app says a new version is ready.
  • Keep your own settings file site/config.js: updates leave it alone.
Do not
  • Do not edit the app code.
  • Do not delete VERSION or LICENSE.
Leaving, or a changed domain
Do
  • Tell your Admin before you change your website address, so the licence can be moved.
  • If you stop, delete your Cloudflare Worker, Supabase project and GitHub repositories, or ask your Admin to delete your account.
  • Your public repository files are cleaned automatically: a deleted or ended account loses them within 3 months at most.
Do not
  • Do not leave an old snippet on a website you no longer manage.
  • Do not share your repository link as proof of your licence.
