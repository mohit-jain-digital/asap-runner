What setup really involves

ASAP-Rank runs in your own free Cloudflare, Supabase and GitHub accounts, so you own your data. That means a one-time setup. Here is exactly what it takes, so you can choose to do it yourself or have us do it.

Do it yourself

About 1 hour the first time

  • Create free GitHub, Cloudflare and Supabase accounts (each needs email verification, no card).
  • Make three access tokens. We show you the exact page and the boxes to tick.
  • Run one command. It creates your repositories, database and app and connects them (about 5 minutes).
  • Create your admin login, then add one snippet to your site (Google Tag Manager, WordPress plugin, Shopify or Wix).
  • Import your sitemap, add an AI key and review the first suggestions.

  • Comfort with copy and paste and one typed command.
  • Everything starts on free plans. Very busy sites may later need a paid plan with the provider (for example Cloudflare Workers Paid at about $5 a month).

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is ASAP-Rank?

ASAP-Rank is a web app that improves a website's search visibility and lead capture. One snippet lets AI suggest titles, descriptions, schema, FAQs and internal links that you approve and publish, while popups, a chatbot, a CRM and Drip messages capture and follow up leads.

What is AEO?

AEO means answer engine optimization. It shapes pages so AI assistants and search answers can quote them: clear question-and-answer sections, FAQ schema and direct, factual wording.

Does it work on my website platform?

Yes. It works on WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Webflow, custom sites and anything that lets you add a script, including through Google Tag Manager. A WordPress plugin is also included.

Can I use it on more than one domain?

Yes. Your first site is included and each extra domain or subdomain is an add-on. Popups, chatbot and Drip messages can be shown on all approved sites or only the ones you choose.

Does it connect to Zoho CRM and HubSpot?

Yes. New leads from popups and the chatbot can be pushed to Zoho CRM and HubSpot, and the built-in CRM also syncs with Google Sheets.

What are MCP and WebMCP?

MCP lets your own AI assistant read your pages and review queue and propose changes for your approval. WebMCP lets a visitor's AI agent use tools you publish for your forms and links.

Can it help with page speed?

Yes. It tracks PageSpeed scores for each page against a baseline and applies the speed rules you approve, such as image priority, lazy loading and delaying heavy third-party scripts.

Are there ready-made popups and chatbot styles?

Yes. Choose from 12 popup templates and 8 chatbot styles, recolour them with ready palettes, then edit the words to fit your business.

What does it run on?

The app runs on Cloudflare with an optional Supabase background engine and a static dashboard on GitHub Pages or your own server, so it can start on free tiers.

What do I need to set up ASAP-Rank?

You need free GitHub, Cloudflare and Supabase accounts, three access tokens, a computer with Node.js to run one installer command, and access to your site's head, Google Tag Manager or WordPress admin. A Google AI Studio key gives you the AI, and Brevo sends emails. The first setup takes about an hour. Setup assistance is available per domain if you would rather have us do it.

Do I need technical skills?

No, but you do need to be comfortable following steps. Setup takes about an hour: you create free GitHub, Cloudflare and Supabase accounts, make three access tokens, and run one installer command that connects everything. Then you add one snippet to your site. If you would rather not, setup assistance is available per domain.

Why is there no free trial?

ASAP-Rank is a site-wide assistance solution, so we would like to work with people who genuinely need it. Our platform fee is deliberately small, and we offer a refund of the platform fee within 7 days of payment if it turns out not to be right for you. If you would like to see it first, try the live demos above, then press Start Now. Setup assistance, if you choose to opt for it, is a one-time service and is not refundable.

What is SGE, and how does ASAP-Rank help with AI Overviews?

SGE (search generative experience) and AI Overviews are AI-written answers shown above search results. ASAP-Rank helps your pages get quoted by adding clear question-and-answer sections, FAQ schema and an llms.txt draft. No tool can promise a placement, but pages without clear answers are rarely quoted.

What are SEO quick wins?

Quick wins are the fastest fixes for each page, such as a missing title or description, duplicate headings, images without alt text or a slow main image. ASAP-Rank lists them in priority order so you fix what matters first.

Can AI write or improve my website content?

Yes. ASAP-Rank can rewrite weak copy, suggest content ideas and draft answers in your tone, using your own AI key. You review and approve every change before it goes live.

What is llms.txt and does ASAP-Rank create it?

llms.txt is a plain file that tells AI assistants what your site is about and which pages matter. ASAP-Rank drafts one for you to review and place in your site root. It is not published automatically.

Do I need AI for the chatbot, and can it capture leads?

No. The chatbot can answer from your own pages and FAQs without AI, and AI answers are optional. It captures the visitor’s name and phone or email, adds them to your CRM and can hand over to you on WhatsApp.

Does ASAP-Rank help with privacy rules like GDPR?

It includes consent capture, one-click unsubscribe and data deletion tools, and your contacts stay in your own accounts. Following the rules depends on how you use it. ASAP-Rank is not a legal service and is not certified by any regulator.

Is there a WordPress plugin?

Yes. Install the ASAP-Rank connector plugin, paste your connection code and press Save and test connection. If you do not use WordPress, one snippet works on Shopify, Wix, Webflow and any site that accepts a script.
