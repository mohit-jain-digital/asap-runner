How it works

You create an account with ASAP-Rank once. Then you put your own copy of the app on your own free Cloudflare account. Your pages, SEO changes, popups, chatbot and contacts stay there, not with us. Your copy asks ASAP-Rank only "is this licence active?" each time someone logs in. The background engine (Step 6) also runs in your own free Supabase and GitHub accounts. We only provide the software and the licence. It takes about 35 minutes in total, and you can do it in parts: this page remembers what you ticked.

Step 1. Create your ASAP-Rank account

  1. Open the sign-up page. Fill the form: first and last name, WhatsApp number with country code (for example 919876543210), email, password (8+ characters), organization, team size, products.
  2. Press Open WhatsApp to get my code. Send the ready message without changing it. Copy the 10-character code after "OTP:" and paste it on the page. The code works once and for 24 hours.
  3. Press Verify and sign up. Your account is created and waits for activation.

Quick setup (recommended): one command

After Step 2, this replaces Steps 3, 4 and 6. The installer makes one public GitHub repository (asap-runner; nothing private, no copy of the app repository), your Supabase project, and your Cloudflare database and app, then connects them and turns on free Pages. Needs Node.js 18 or newer (free, from nodejs.org).

  1. Make the three free accounts in Step 3 (GitHub, Cloudflare, Supabase). Email verification only, no card.
  2. Press and unzip it (or use the setup folder your Admin sent you, if it has install.mjs). Double-click Install-ASAP-Rank-Customer-Windows.bat (Windows) or Install-ASAP-Rank-Customer-Mac.command (Mac). A black window opens and asks questions (your licence key, three tokens). If Node.js is missing it tries to install it and tells you to double-click again. Mac: if it says the file cannot be opened, right-click it, choose Open, then Open. Prefer typing? Run this in a terminal in that folder:
node install.mjs customer
  1. It asks for three tokens and prints the exact page to open for each: GitHub (tick repo and workflow), Supabase, and Cloudflare (permissions: Workers Scripts Edit, D1 Edit, Account Settings Read). Paste each and press Enter.
  2. When it prints DONE, do Step 5 (create your admin login) and Step 7 (add your pages and the snippet).

Still by hand: creating the accounts, verifying emails, making the three tokens, your admin login and the snippet on your website. To update later, run the same command again. Prefer clicking through? Skip this card and do Steps 3 to 6. If this feels like too much, ask your Admin about setup assistance.

Step 3. Free accounts

  1. Create a free GitHub account (or log in).
  2. Create a free Cloudflare account (or log in). No card is needed for the free plan.
  3. Create a free Supabase account (or log in). It is the database for the background engine in Step 6.

Step 4. Deploy your own copy

Paste the app code yourself. No GitHub copy is needed for this step.

  1. In Cloudflare, left menu: Storage & databases, then D1 SQL database, then Create database. Name it asap-rank-data and click Create. Leave it empty: the app builds its own tables.
  2. Left menu: Compute (Workers), then Workers & Pages, then Create, then start with Hello World. Name it asap-rank and click Deploy.
  3. Click Edit code. Click inside the code area, press Ctrl+A (Cmd+A on Mac), then Delete.
  4. Press the button below, paste into the empty editor, then click Deploy (top right).
  5. Open the app, then Settings, then Bindings, then Add. Choose D1 database. Variable name DB (capital letters). Database asap-rank-data. Save.
  6. Still in Settings, open Variables and secrets and add these four. LICENSE_KEY, SECRET and SETUP_CODE are type Secret; HUB_URL is type Text. Without HUB_URL your app shows the ASAP-Rank sales page instead of your login, then click Deploy:
LICENSE_KEY
HUB_URL (type Text, not Secret): your ASAP-Rank sign-up address
SECRET (a random password for the app)
SETUP_CODE (you type it once when you create your admin login; write it down)
  1. Your app address looks like https://asap-rank.something.workers.dev. Note it down for Step 5. If you update later, repeat points 3 and 4 only.
  1. Press Deploy to Cloudflare. Connect GitHub when asked: it makes a copy in your GitHub and a database in your Cloudflare.
  2. In the form, enter these values. Use the buttons to copy them:
LICENSE_KEY
HUB_URL (type Text, not Secret): your ASAP-Rank sign-up address
SECRET (a random password for the app)
SETUP_CODE (you type it once when you create your admin login; write it down)

Optional values (you can add them later in Cloudflare, Workers, your app, Settings, Variables): BREVO_KEY or RESEND_KEY to send email, MAIL_FROM as Your name <you@yourdomain.com>, GEMINI_KEY for the AI, PSI_KEY for speed tests. Use your own keys.

  1. Press Create and deploy and wait until it says deployed. Note your app address: it looks like https://asap-rank.something.workers.dev.

Step 5. Create your admin login

  1. Open your app address. You see Create your admin login.
  2. Use the same email you used in Step 1 and choose a password.
  3. Type the setup code: the one the installer printed at the end, or the SETUP_CODE you added in Cloudflare. Without it nobody can create the admin login, even if they know your address. Your app then checks your licence and opens.
  4. From now on log in with email or WhatsApp number and password at your app address.

Step 8. Get your first improvements

  1. Add an AI model: More, AI models. A free Google Gemini key from Google AI Studio works.
  2. In All URLs tick a few pages and press AI suggestions for selected, or press AI quick wins for the pages with the most problems first. Suggestions appear within a few minutes.
  3. Open a page. Read each suggestion, edit it if you like, then press Approve and Publish now. Revoke takes a change off your site again.

Step 9. Lead capture, messages and payment

  1. Settings, Messaging: connect your email service. Marketing: popups, chatbot, drip messages and CRM (all included in your plan).
  2. To activate your account: More, Plan, and pay with the details shown. Send the transaction ID by replying to the welcome email. Your app picks up the activation at your next login.

Step 10. Using ASAP-Rank every week

  1. Home: see how your checks score, page speed and search position changed this week and this month. The first comparison appears after a week.
  2. All URLs: import your sitemap, press Check all new, tick pages and press AI suggestions for selected (or AI quick wins).
  3. Open a page: edit, Approve, Publish now. Revoke removes a change again.
  4. Review: everything waiting in one list. Turn on Auto-publish for the safe types after a waiting time you choose.
  5. After publishing, wait a few minutes (up to about 10). The free files refresh in the background.
  6. Marketing: popups, chatbot, drip messages and CRM. Leads from popups and chat land in CRM.
  7. Once a month: re-import the sitemap for new pages, and upload the same Search Console export so search position shows progress.
