Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is ASAP-Rank?

ASAP-Rank is a web app that improves a website's search visibility and lead capture. One snippet lets AI suggest titles, descriptions, schema, FAQs and internal links that you approve and publish, while popups, a chatbot, a CRM and drip messages capture and follow up leads.

What is AEO?

AEO means answer engine optimization. It shapes pages so AI assistants and search answers can quote them: clear question-and-answer sections, FAQ schema and direct, factual wording.

Does it work on my website platform?

Yes. It works on WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Webflow, custom sites and anything that lets you add a script, including through Google Tag Manager. A WordPress plugin is also included.

Can I use it on more than one domain?

Yes. Your first site is included and each extra domain or subdomain is an add-on. Popups, chatbot and drip messages can be shown on all approved sites or only the ones you choose.

Does it connect to Zoho CRM and HubSpot?

Yes. New leads from popups and the chatbot can be pushed to Zoho CRM and HubSpot, and the built-in CRM also syncs with Google Sheets.

What are MCP and WebMCP?

MCP lets your own AI assistant read your pages and review queue and propose changes for your approval. WebMCP lets a visitor's AI agent use tools you publish for your forms and links.

Can it help with page speed?

Yes. It tracks PageSpeed scores for each page against a baseline and applies the speed rules you approve, such as image priority, lazy loading and delaying heavy third-party scripts.

Are there ready-made popups and chatbot styles?

Yes. Choose from 12 popup templates and 8 chatbot styles, recolour them with ready palettes, then edit the words to fit your business.

What does it run on?

The app runs on Cloudflare with an optional Supabase background engine and a static dashboard on GitHub Pages or your own server, so it can start on free tiers.

Do I need technical skills?

No. You paste one snippet, approve the changes you like and press publish. Setup assistance is available per domain if you want us to do it for you.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new account gets a 7-day free trial with no card needed.
